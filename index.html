Bhagavad Gita — Chapter 2, Verse 47

Meter: Anuṣṭubh Chhandas

✦ Original Sanskrit Shloka

कर्मण्येवाधिकारस्ते मा फलेषु कदाचन।
मा कर्मफलहेतुर्भूर्मा ते सङ्गोऽस्त्वकर्मणि॥

✦ IAST Roman Transliteration

karmaṇy-evādhikāras te mā phaleṣu kadācana |
mā karma-phala-hetur bhūr mā te saṅgo 'stv akarmaṇi ||

✦ English Translation

You have a right only to perform your prescribed duty, but never to the fruits of action. Never consider yourself the cause of the results of your activities, and never be attached to inaction.

Introduction to Sanskrit

Known as Devavani (the language of the gods), Sanskrit is an extraordinarily precise phonetic language designed to produce vibrational resonances that harmonize cognitive state and consciousness.

  • 50 Aksharas: Perfectly ordered by anatomical point of vocal contact.
  • Devanagari Script: "City of Divine Light" syllabic alphabet.
  • Sonic Vibrations: Chanting aligns brainwave coherence and memory.
गीता

Understanding Bhagavad Gita

A 700-verse philosophical conversation between Sri Krishna and Arjuna on the battlefield of Kurukshetra, synthesizing three immortal paths of spiritual liberation.

  • Karma Yoga: The path of selfless, detached, and dedicated action.
  • Bhakti Yoga: The path of unconditional divine love and surrender.
  • Jnana Yoga: The path of discrimination between transient and eternal.
सूत्र

Sanskrit Grammar Basics

Structured by the ancient grammarian Panini into the Ashtadhyayi, Sanskrit grammar acts like an exact algebraic algorithm with rigorous inflection rules.

  • 8 Vibhaktis (Cases): Prathama (Nominative) through Sambodhana (Vocative).
  • Dhatus (Verbal Roots): Over 2,000 root seeds creating all nouns and verbs.
  • Sandhi (Euphonic Junctions): Sound fusion rules between adjoining words.
